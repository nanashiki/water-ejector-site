# Water Ejector

スピーカーに水が入って音がこもるときに、低い音を鳴らして水を押し出すのを助ける iPhone・iPad アプリです。

When water gets into your speaker and the sound turns muffled, Water Ejector plays a low tone to help push the water out. For iPhone and iPad.
